## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-10

### Added
- Initial production release of **SpellBee** by ViveScript Solutions LLC.
- Curated offline word bank featuring Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced difficulty tiers.
- Multiple-choice and Missing-Letter interactive spelling quizzes.
- On-device speech pronunciation using Android's native `TextToSpeech` engine with slow-speech (0.75x) toggle.
- Letter-by-letter "Spell Aloud" audio feature.
- Instant encouraging feedback with celebratory bee animations and mnemonic tips for tricky spellings.
- Scoring system with streak multipliers (up to 2.0x points).
- One-tap native social score sharing (WhatsApp, Instagram Stories, TikTok, Messages).
- Session word review screen with word definitions, phonetic guides, and audio replay.
- Centralized `AdConfig` configured with Google Mobile Ads SDK (`23.6.0`), App ID `ca-app-pub-5222053984568989~4545635611`, Banner 1 `ca-app-pub-5222053984568989/4396452645`, and Interstitial 1 `ca-app-pub-5222053984568989/4865199819`, fully compliant with COPPA and Google Play Families Policies.
- Comprehensive local unit tests and Robolectric tests for word validation and preferences.
- Google Play Store assets package including 512x512 app icon, 1024x500 feature graphic, and screenshots.
- Complete legal and compliance documentation (MIT License with proprietary branding notice, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Play Store Metadata, and Data Safety).
