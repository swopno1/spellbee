## 1. Overview

ViveScript Solutions LLC ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of our users. **SpellBee** is designed as a lightweight, educational English spelling quiz application that respects user privacy and functions offline without requiring accounts, logins, or personal profile creation.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

- **No Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or contact information.
- **No Account Registration:** The application requires no login, sign-up, or third-party authentication.
- **No Biometric or Voice Recording:** The app utilizes Android's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine to output spoken pronunciation. No user voice or audio is recorded or transmitted.
- **No Precise Location:** We do not request or access GPS or precise location data.
- **No Contacts or Media Files:** The app does not access your contacts, camera, or external media storage.

---

## 3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide an engaging and rewarding gameplay experience, SpellBee stores non-identifying game preferences locally on your device via standard Android SharedPreferences:
- High score and point totals
- Best answer streaks
- Total quizzes played and words spelled
- Audio settings (auto-pronunciation toggle, speech playback rate)

This data is stored solely on your local device and is never uploaded to any remote server or third party. You can clear this data at any time through Android Settings > Apps > SpellBee > Clear Storage.

---

## 4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

SpellBee incorporates Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to support the ongoing development of educational utilities.
- **Child & Family Safety Compliance (COPPA):** The application is configured using `TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT_TRUE` and a maximum content rating of `G`.
- **Test Configuration:** Current builds utilize official Google Mobile Ads test identifiers.
- **Data Safety:** For full details on Google Mobile Ads privacy practices, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy at [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

SpellBee is intended for general audiences, including children, students, and language learners. We strictly comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 6. Social Sharing

SpellBee includes a one-tap "Share Score" feature that utilizes Android's native sharing sheet (`Intent.ACTION_SEND`). When you tap Share Score, the app passes a pre-formatted text message to your chosen messaging or social media application (e.g., WhatsApp, Instagram, Messages). SpellBee does not transmit this data independently or access your social media accounts.

---

## 7. Security

Because SpellBee operates offline and does not store personal information on external servers, the risk of data compromise is minimal. All local application data is protected by Android’s application sandboxing security model.
